This repository is a proof of concept for engineering evaluation. It is not a released product, medical-device implementation, safety mechanism, validated test system, production data-acquisition system, or certification of conformance.

The 0.2.0 candidate records Project-local adoption of five engineering documents from `host-device-control-framework`; those documents remain `Draft for Review`. Automated checks cover selected structural rules only. A controlled Windows build, test evidence, physical-device validation, and human approval remain required.

Human engineers remain responsible for:

- defining and approving requirements;
- reviewing authority, protocol, architecture, code, deviations, and evidence;
- confirming electrical, driver, resource, and timing assumptions;
- verifying real device behavior and failure handling;
- performing risk analysis and product verification;
- approving any reuse in another system.
